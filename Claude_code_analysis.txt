  My Recommendation for Modularizing the World

  Based on my analysis, here's the path forward:

  DON'T try to revive the campaign system - it was abandoned for good reasons and would take 6-9 weeks with high
  risk to your 417 passing tests.

  DO consider these incremental approaches:

  1. Modular Dungeon Regions (Safest)
    - Create thematic dungeon clusters (Mountain Dungeons, Swamp Dungeons, etc.)
    - Link them with simple "travel menu" (no hex map needed)
    - Preserve dungeon-focused gameplay
    - No parser changes, minimal risk
  2. Quest-Based Connections (Medium)
    - Add quest chains that guide players between dungeons
    - NPCs in villages give quests pointing to specific dungeons
    - Natural narrative flow without complex overworld
    - Builds on existing village system
  3. Hub-and-Spoke Model (Best Balance)
    - One "hub town" with multiple dungeon entrances
    - Each dungeon is a separate module/scenario
    - Simple fast-travel between hub and dungeons
    - Preserves testing, adds world feel

  All three approaches avoid the mistakes of the campaign attempt while still providing a more cohesive world feel.
