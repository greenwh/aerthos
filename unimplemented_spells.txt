CLERIC
================================================================================


Level 1 Spells (7 unimplemented):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  • Command (enchantment)
    Give a one-word command that the target must obey (flee, halt, drop, etc).

  • Create Water (unknown)
    Generates 4 gallons of clean water per level of the caster. **Reverse:** *Destroy Water* obliterates a like quantity of water.

  • Detect Evil (divination)
    Detects evil intentions and evil creatures.

  • Purify Food & Drink (unknown)
    Makes up to 1 cubic foot of spoiled/poisoned food or water safe to consume. **Reverse:** *Putrefy Food & Drink* spoils safe food/water.

  • Remove Fear (unknown)
    Instills courage, granting a +4 bonus to saving throws against magical fear for 1 turn. If affected by fear, grants another save with a +1/level bonus. **Reverse:** *Cause Fear* causes a victim to flee in panic.

  • Resist Cold (unknown)
    Creature touched is inured to cold, receiving a +3 bonus to saving throws and taking half (or quarter on successful save) damage from greater cold attacks (e.g., *ice storm*, dragon breath).

  • Sanctuary (unknown)
    Opponents must make a saving throw vs. magic to strike or attack the protected cleric. Cleric cannot take offensive action while protected.


Level 2 Spells (7 unimplemented):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  • Augury (unknown)
    Seeks to divine whether a future action (within 3 turns) will be beneficial or harmful. Base chance of 70% success + 1%/level.

  • Chant (unknown)
    Gives a +1 bonus to attack rolls, damage, and saving throws for friendly creatures within a 3" radius for as long as the cleric chants. Enemy rolls are penalized by -1.

  • Detect Charm (unknown)
    Detects if a creature is under the influence of a *charm* spell (up to 10 creatures). **Reverse:** *Obscure Charm* protects a single creature from such detection.

  • Know Alignment (unknown)
    Reveals the exact alignment of up to 10 persons. **Reverse:** *Obscure Alignment* totally obscures the alignment of a single person for 1 turn.

  • Resist Fire (unknown)
    Creature touched is toughened to withstand heat, taking half (or quarter on successful save) damage from magical fires (e.g., *fireball*, dragon breath) and gaining a +3 bonus to saving throws vs. fire attacks.

  • Snake Charm (unknown)
    Causes one or more snakes to cease activity and sway hypnotically. Total hit points of snakes affected cannot exceed the cleric's level.

  • Speak With Animals (unknown)
    Enables the cleric to communicate with any warm or cold-blooded animal (not mindless). Lasts 2 rounds per level.


Level 3 Spells (9 unimplemented):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  • Animate Dead (unknown)
    Creates skeletons or zombies from dead human remains to obey the cleric's commands (1 monster/level).

  • Create Food & Water (unknown)
    Creates 1 cubic foot of nourishing food and/or water per level, sustaining three human-sized creatures for a full day.

  • Feign Death (unknown)
    Puts the touched creature into a cataleptic state, appearing dead.

  • Glyph of Warding (evoc.)
    Inscribes a magical rune to guard an area (25 sq. ft. per level) which, when triggered, releases a stored effect (e.g., electrical damage, fire damage, paralyze).

  • Protection from Evil 10' Radius (abjuration)
    As Protection from Evil, but affects all within 10 feet of caster.

  • Remove Curse (abjuration)
    Removes one curse from creature or object.

  • Speak With Dead (unknown)
    Allows the cleric to ask several questions of a dead creature (body or remains must be present). Limits on questions and maximum time dead depend on cleric level.

  • Tongues (unknown)
    Enables the caster to speak the language of any creature (same as Cleric 4th Level).

  • Word of Recall (unknown)
    Instantly teleports the cleric back to his or her previously designated sanctuary, carrying additional weight (250 gp/level) in equipment, treasure, or persons.


Level 4 Spells (6 unimplemented):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  • Detect Lie (rev.)
    Enables the recipient to determine if truth is being spoken (lasts 1 round/level). Reverse: *Undetectable Lie* makes a lie seem reasonable.

  • Divination (divination)
    Gives information regarding an area (small woods, building, dungeon level). Success chance 60% + 1%/level, adjusted by DM for area specifics.

  • Exorcise (divination)
    Negates possession of a creature or object by an outside/supernatural force (e.g., demon, curse, charm, *magic jar*). Base 1-100% chance of success, checked each turn.

  • Lower Water (rev.)
    Causes water or similar fluid in a 1" x 1" square/level area to sink away (5% of original effect/level). Reverse: *Raise Water* causes water to return to its normal highest level + 1 foot/level.

  • Speak With Plants (divination)
    Enables the cleric to converse with any living vegetable, allowing questions about creatures, making thickets part, etc. (lasts 1 round/level).

  • Sticks to Snakes (rev.)
    Changes 1 stick/level into a snake (5% chance/level of being venomous) that attacks as commanded. Reverse: *Snakes to Sticks* changes snakes to sticks.


Level 5 Spells (7 unimplemented):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  • Atonement (divination)
    Removes the onus of unwilling or unknown deeds, or magical alignment change. Requires true repentance.

  • Commune (divination)
    Contacts the cleric's divinity (or agents) to request information in the form of questions answered by a simple "yes" or "no" (1 question/level).

  • Dispel Evil (rev.)
    Causes summoned/enchanted evil creatures to return to their own plane or place. Affected creatures attack the caster at -7 penalty. Reverse: *Dispel Good* against summoned/enchanted good creatures.

  • Insect Plague (divination)
    Horde of insects swarms in a 36" diameter, 6" high cloud, dealing 1 HP damage/round to creatures within, and forcing morale checks.

  • Plane Shift (divination)
    Moves the cleric (and up to 6 others linked in a circle) to another plane of existence. The specific destination depends on a small, forked metal rod component.

  • Quest (enchantment)
    Requires an affected creature to perform a service and return with proof. Failure results in -1 penalty to saving throws daily.

  • True Seeing (rev.)
    Allows the cleric to see things as they actually are in a 12" sight range, revealing secret doors, invisible things, illusions, and creature auras. Reverse: *False Seeing* causes a person to see things as they are not.


Level 6 Spells (7 unimplemented):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  • Aerial Servant (conjuration)
    Summons an invisible stalker to perform the cleric's bidding for a maximum of 1 day/level, primarily for finding and returning objects.

  • Animate Object (divination)
    Imbues inanimate objects (1 cubic foot/level) with mobility and a semblance of life to attack as commanded.

  • Conjure Animals (divination)
    Summons one or more mammals with total HD up to the cleric's level to attack opponents. Lasts 2 rounds/level.

  • Find The Path (rev.)
    Enables the cleric to find the shortest, most direct route to a known destination (outdoors, underground, trap, or *maze* spell). Reverse: *Lose the Path* makes the creature touched totally lost.

  • Part Water (divination)
    Causes water or similar liquid to move apart, forming a trough 3' deep by 1' wide by 2" long/level.

  • Speak With Monsters (divination)
    Allows the cleric to converse with any type of creature with communicative ability (lasts 1 melee round/level).

  • Stone Tell (divination)
    The very stones of an area (1 cubic yard of stone) speak and relate what has touched them, what is concealed, and what has transpired.


Level 7 Spells (9 unimplemented):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  • Astral Spell (divination)
    Projects the cleric's astral body into the Astral Plane, leaving the physical body behind in suspended animation. Allows travel to other planes.

  • Control Weather (divination)
    Allows the cleric to change the weather in an area of 4-16 square miles (takes 1 turn to cast, 1-4 turns for effect).

  • Earthquake (divination)
    Creates a local tremor in a circular area (1/2" diameter/level), causing cliffs/caves to collapse, ground to crack, and structures to sustain damage/fall.

  • Gate (divination)
    Opens an ultra-dimensional connection to a specific powerful being (demon, devil, demi-god, god, etc.), causing it to step through the portal or be attracted.

  • Holy (Unholy) Word (divination)
    Utterance has tremendous power, driving off evil (or good for *Unholy Word*) creatures from other planes, and affecting other creatures of differing alignment (killing, paralyzing, stunning, deafening) based on their HD/level.

  • Restoration (rev.)
    Raises the life energy level of the recipient creature by one, subsuming previous life energy level drain. Reverse: *Energy Drain* draws away one life energy level (requires touch attack).

  • Resurrection (rev.)
    Restores life and complete strength to a dead person (subject to Constitution survival save) dead up to 10 years/level. Reverse: *Destruction* causes the victim to be instantly dead and turned to dust.

  • Symbol (conjuration)
    Inscribes magical runes that, when triggered, affect creatures passing over/touching/reading/passing through the portal with a chosen effect (Death, Discord, Fear, Hopelessness).

  • Wind Walk (divination)
    Alters the cleric's body (and up to two others touched) to cloud-like vapors, allowing travel at up to 60" per turn (not invisible, but appear misty). ***

Level 1 Spells (9 unimplemented):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  • Animal Friendship (unknown)
    Charms one animal (non-semi-intelligent) to be a trusted friend and ally of the druid (subject to save). Allows druid to teach it 3 "tricks"/INT point.

  • Detect Snares & Pits (unknown)
    Detects hidden snares and pits (simple traps only underground, all traps outdoors) in a 1" path up to 4" long.

  • Entangle (unknown)
    Causes plants (*grasses, weeds, trees*) in a 4" diameter area to wrap, twist, and entwine creatures, holding them fast or slowing movement by 50% (subject to save).

  • Faerie Fire (unknown)
    Outlines an object or creature with a pale glowing light, making it visible (even if invisible) and giving a +2 penalty to opponents' AC ("to hit" bonus).

  • Invisibility To Animals (unknown)
    Makes the recipient totally undetectable by normal animals (INT under 6).

  • Locate Animals (unknown)
    Determines the direction and distance of any desired type of animal within range.

  • Pass Without Trace (unknown)
    Recipient moves through any terrain without leaving footprint or scent, making tracking impossible.

  • Predict Weather (unknown)
    Gains 100% accurate knowledge of the weather trend (sky, temperature, precipitation) for 2 hours in advance per level of the caster in a 9 square mile area.

  • Shillelagh (unknown)
    Changes a normal oaken club into a magical +1 weapon, inflicting 2-8 HP (man-sized) or 2-5 HP (larger).


Level 2 Spells (7 unimplemented):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  • Charm Person Or Mammal (unknown)
    Charms one person or mammal (not monster) to regard the druid as a trusted friend and ally (subject to save). Duration depends on the charmed creature's intelligence.

  • Fire Trap (evoc.)
    Traps a closable object (e.g., door, chest) to explode with fire damage (1-4 HP, half with save) when opened/touched by an unauthorized creature.

  • Heat Metal (unknown)
    Causes ferrous metal (iron, steel) in a mass of 500 gp/level to become searing hot over 7 rounds, causing damage and disability to exposed flesh. **Reverse:** *Chill Metal* causes metal to become freezing, causing damage and amputation.

  • Obscurement (unknown)
    Causes a misty vapor to arise around the druid, reducing visibility to 2' to 8' (2d4) for 4 rounds per level.

  • Produce Flame (unknown)
    Creates a bright, hot flame in the druid's palm. Lasts 2 rounds/level. Can be hurled as a missile (range 4"), igniting combustibles.

  • Trip (charm)
    Causes a stick, vine, or rope object to rise and trip creatures crossing it (subject to save). Creatures running take damage and are stunned.

  • Warp Wood (unknown)
    Permanently bends and warps wood in a 1" range/level, destroying its straightness, form, and strength.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Calls down bolts of lightning from the sky (must be a storm) that inflict 2d8+1d8/level HP damage (half with save) to creatures within a 1" radius of the strike.

  • Hold Animal (charm)
    Holds 1-4 animals rigid (mammals, birds, or reptiles, not monsters) for 2 rounds/level. Saving throw is penalized based on the number of targets.

  • Plant Growth (unknown)
    Causes normal vegetation (brush, trees, vines) in a 2" X 2" square area/level to become a dense barrier.

  • Protection From Fire (unknown)
    Grants invulnerability to normal fires and 50% damage reduction (+4 save bonus) against magical fires for 1 turn/level.

  • Pyrotechnics (unknown)
    Causes a fire source to produce either a flashing burst of fireworks (blinding creatures) or a thick stream of smoke (obscuring vision).

  • Snare (charm)
    Creates a 90% undetectable snare from a flexible object (vine, rope) that contracts to bind or strangle a creature (1-6 HP damage) when triggered.

  • Stone Shape (unknown)
    Permanently forms an existing piece of stone (3 cubic feet/level) into a desired shape (e.g., weapon, trapdoor, idol).

  • Summon Insects (unknown)
    Attracts a cloud of flying or crawling insects (70% chance of flying) that swarm and attack a target, dealing 2 HP damage/round, and forcing the victim to fend them off.

  • Tree (unknown)
    Allows the druid to assume the form of a small living tree or shrub, or a large dead tree trunk, and observe their surroundings while in the form.

  • Water Breathing (unknown)
    Allows the recipient to freely breathe underwater for 6 turns/level. **Reverse:** *Air Breathing* allows water-breathing creatures to survive in the atmosphere.

================================================================================
ILLUSIONIST
================================================================================


Level 1 Spells (4 unimplemented):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  • Audible Glamer (phantasm)
    Creates a volume of sound (talking, singing, roaring, etc.) at whatever range the caster desires (up to a dragon's roar).

  • Change Self (phantasm)
    Alters the appearance of the illusionist's form, including clothing and equipment, to any generally man-shaped bipedal creature.

  • Gaze Reflection (alt.)
    Creates a mirror-like area of air that reflects back any gaze attack (e.g., basilisk, medusa) upon the gazer.

  • Wall Of Fog (alt.)
    Creates a wall of misty vapors that obscures all sight (normal or infravision) beyond 2'.


Level 2 Spells (4 unimplemented):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  • Blindness (phantasm)
    Causes the recipient creature to become totally blind (no cure except *dispel magic* or caster's will).

  • Hypnotic Pattern (phantasm)
    Creates a weaving, turning pattern of subtle colors that causes creatures looking at it to become fascinated and entranced (up to 24 HD/levels total).

  • Improved Phantasmal Force (phantasm)
    A phantasmal force illusion that can be maintained with minimal concentration (lasts 2 rounds after concentration ceases).

  • Phantasmal Force (phantasm)
    Creates a visual illusion of an object, creature, or force (4 square" + 1 square"/level). Creatures believe it and can suffer damage until they successfully disbelieve.


Level 3 Spells (5 unimplemented):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  • Dispel Illusion (abj.)
    Dispels any *phantasmal force* (with or without audible glamer) cast by a non-illusionist, and has a chance to dispel other illusionist spells.

  • Illusionary Script (phantasm)
    Creates illusionary script on paper/parchment that can only be read by the person(s) the illusionist desires (others become confused).

  • Invisibility, 10' Radius (phantasm)
    Makes the creature touched and all its gear within a 10' radius invisible.

  • Paralyzation (phantasm)
    Creates illusionary muscle slowdown that paralyzes creatures whose total HD do not exceed twice the illusionist's level.

  • Spectral Force (phantasm)
    Creates an illusion that includes sound, smell, and thermal illusions.


Level 4 Spells (3 unimplemented):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  • Dispel Exhaustion (phantasm)
    Gives the illusion that a touched person is fresh and well, restoring 50% of lost HP.

  • Emotion (enchantment)
    Projects a chosen emotion (Fear, Hate, Hopelessness, Rage) from the illusionist, affecting morale, attack rolls, damage, or saving throws.

  • Phantasmal Killer (phantasm)
    Creates the illusion of the victim's most fearsome imagined thing. On a successful hit, the victim dies from fright.


Level 5 Spells (3 unimplemented):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  • Demi-Shadow Monsters (phantasm)
    Creates quasi-real monsters (40% real) of armor class 8 with 40% of normal hit points and damage potential.

  • Major Creation (alt.)
    Creates mineral objects (same as *Minor Creation* for vegetable objects, but permanent).

  • Shadow Magic (phantasm)
    Allows the caster to cast a quasi-real M-U spell (*magic missile, fireball, lightning bolt, or cone of cold*) that deals damage (or 1 HP/level if recognized).


Level 6 Spells (6 unimplemented):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  • Demi-Shadow Magic (phantasm)
    Allows the caster to cast quasi-real M-U spells (*wall of fire, wall of ice, or cloudkill*) that deal more damage than *Shadow Magic*.

  • Mass Suggestion (enchantment)
    Same as M-U 3rd Level, but affects one creature per level of the illusionist.

  • Permanent Illusion (phantasm)
    Creates a lasting *spectral force* (same as *Phantasmal Force* but permanent and requires no concentration).

  • Shades (phantasm)
    Creates quasi-real monsters (60% real) of armor class 6 with 60% of normal hit points and damage potential.

  • True Sight (divination)
    Allows the illusionist to see its actual or former form, but does not allow determination of alignment.

  • Veil (phantasm)
    Instantly changes the appearance of surroundings (up to 2" X 2"/level) to fool creatures, making a filthy den look like a sumptuous room.


Level 7 Spells (4 unimplemented):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  • Alter Reality (phantasm, conj.)
    Similar to *Limited Wish*, allowing the illusionist to alter reality through the casting of a *phantasmal force* illusion.

  • Prismatic Spray (abj., conj.)
    Causes 7 rays of the *prismatic sphere* spell (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet) to spring from the caster's hand, affecting creatures with a random effect (based on d8 roll).

  • Prismatic Wall (abj., conj.)
    Creates a wall, or curtain, of scintillating colors with effects identical to the *prismatic sphere* globes, with maximum proportions 4' wide per level and 2' high per level.

  • Vision (divination)
    Allows the illusionist to call upon a power for supernatural guidance, receiving a *vision* in answer to a question (chance of success based on dice roll, intelligence, and sacrifice).
