	
  • Delayed Blast Fireball (evocation)
    Same as *Fireball*, but +1 on each damage die, and can be commanded to release its blast up to 5 rounds after casting.

  • Mass Invisibility (phantasm)
    Makes up to 300-400 man-sized creatures invisible within a 3" X 3" area.

  • Power Word, Stun (conjuration)
    Stuns one creature with up to 90 HP for 1-8 rounds, with duration depending on the victim's current HP.

  • Incendiary Cloud (evocation)
    Creates a dense vapor cloud (10' high, 20' long/wide) that begins to flame on the 3rd round, dealing 1/2 HP damage/level to creatures inside.

  • Mass Charm (enchantment)
    Charms a number of creatures (total HD not exceeding twice the caster's level) in a maximum 3" X 3" area.

  • Bigby's Clenched Fist (evocation)
    Creates a huge, disembodied hand of force that strikes an opponent each round, with varying degrees of damage/stunning (1-6 HP up to 4-24 HP).
	
  • Power Word, Blind (conjuration)
    Temporarily blinds one or more creatures (up to 100 HP total affected) for 2-5 rounds or turns, with duration based on current HP.
	
  • Monster Summoning VI (conjuration)
    Summons 1 or 2 sixth level monsters.

  • Otto's Irresistible Dance (enchantment)
    Causes the recipient creature to begin dancing uncontrollably, lowering their AC by -4, making saves impossible, and negating shield use.
	
  • Statue (divination)
    Turns the caster or another creature (and gear) into solid stone (subject to save), where they can still see/hear/smell.

  • Monster Summoning V (conjuration)
    Summons 1-2 fifth level monsters.

  • Mordenkainen's Sword (evocation)
    Brings into being a shimmering sword-like plane of force that strikes as a fighter of half the caster's level, dealing 5-20 HP damage.
	
  • Resurrection (rev.) and the reverse
    Restores life and complete strength to a dead person (subject to Constitution survival save) dead up to 10 years/level. Reverse: *Destruction* causes the victim to be instantly dead and turned to dust.

  • Water Breathing (unknown)
    Allows the recipient to freely breathe underwater for 6 turns/level. **Reverse:** *Air Breathing* allows water-breathing creatures to survive in the atmosphere.

  • Remove Curse (abjuration)
    Removes one curse from creature or object.
	
  • Charm Monster (enchantment)
    Charms any living creature (or several of lesser level) to regard the caster as friendly (same as 1st level, but more powerful).
	
  • Fire Shield (evocation)
    Immolates the caster (but no damage taken) with flames, protecting against cold-based attacks (half damage, +2 save bonus) and dealing double damage to body/hand-held weapon attackers.
	
  • Monster Summoning II (conjuration)
    Summons 1-6 second level monsters.
	
  • Airy Water (divination)
    Turns normal liquid to a less dense, breathable substance in a 2" diameter sphere/4" diameter hemisphere, allowing movement and breathing underwater.

  • Bigby's Interposing Hand (evocation)
    Creates a large, disembodied hand of force that interposes itself between the caster and opponent, taking damage as the caster.

  • Chaos (enchantment)
    Causes all creatures in a 4" X 4" area to become confused (same as 4th level, but affects all creatures).

  • Conjure Elemental (divination)
    Summons a 16 HD Air, Earth, Fire, or Water Elemental to do the caster's bidding (requires concentration, 5% chance of turning on caster).

  • Hold Monster (enchantment)
    Immobilizes 1-4 creatures of any type (monsters/humans) in sight and range for 1 round/level.

  • Monster Summoning III (conjuration)
    Summons 1-4 third level monsters.

  • Wall Of Force (evocation)
    Creates an invisible barrier (20' square/level) that is totally impervious to all magic, spells, blows, and missiles (destroyed only by *disintegrate*).

  • Wall Of Iron (evocation)
    Creates a vertical iron wall (1/4" thick/level, 15 sq. ft./level) that inserts itself into surrounding material. Permanent unless dispelled or destroyed by force.

  • Wall Of Stone (evocation)
    Creates a wall of granite rock (1/4" thick, 20' square/level) that merges into adjoining rock surfaces. Permanent unless destroyed by *disintegrate* or force.

  • Death Spell (conjuration)
    Instantly and irrevocably slays creatures with less than 2 HD, or 4-80 (4d20) HD of creatures with 2-8 HD in a 1/2" square/level area.
	
  • Globe Of Invulnerability (abj.)
    Creates a magical sphere around the caster which prevents any 1st through 4th level spells from penetrating.
	
	
  • Stone To Flesh (rev.) and reverse
    Turns any sort of stone (9 cubic feet/level) into flesh (restoring life if formerly living). Reverse: *Flesh to Stone* turns flesh of any sort to stone (subject to save).

  • Tenser's Transformation (evocation)  
    Undergoes a startling transformation, doubling hit points and attack rate, improving AC by 4, and granting +2 damage on attacks (lasts 1 round/level).

  • Wish (conjuration)
    A more potent version of *Limited Wish*, which can fulfill the caster's utterance literally, but can still cause disaster if phrased poorly. ***
	
  • Shape Change (divination)
    Allows the caster to assume the form of any creature (short of a demi-god, demon prince, etc.) and all its physical abilities, over and over (1 turn/level).

  • Temporal Stasis (rev.)
    Places the recipient creature into a state of suspended animation, where time stops for them (no aging/body functions). Reverse: *Temporal Reinstatement* removes the stasis.
