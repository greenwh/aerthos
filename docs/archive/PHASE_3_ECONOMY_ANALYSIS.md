## ⚠️ IDENTIFIED BALANCE ISSUES

### 1. **CRITICAL: Episode 2 Has Zero Dungeon Gold** 🔴

**Current State:**
- Episode 2 (Oakhaven Sewers): 0 gp dungeon loot
- Players receive only 150 gp episode bonus
- Total income: 150 gp (lowest in campaign)

**Impact:**
- Players coming off Episode 1 (250 gp) hit a wall
- Cannot afford chain mail (75 gp) or equipment upgrades
- Healing potion budget is extremely tight (50 gp each)
- Resource management becomes punishing rather than challenging

**Recommended Fix:**
- Add 300-500 gp in dungeon loot across Oakhaven Sewers
- Distribute as 3-5 treasure caches (100-150 gp each)
- Place in boss room, hidden areas, or quest rewards
- **Target Total:** 450-650 gp for Episode 2 (300-500 dungeon + 150 bonus)

---

### 2. **MAJOR: Massive Wealth Spike in Episodes 9-10** 🟡

**Current State:**
- Episodes 9-10: 15,800 gp combined (50.4% of total campaign gold)
- Episode 9: 8,450 gp
- Episode 10: 9,350 gp

**Impact:**
- Players suddenly have more gold than they can spend
- Can buy unlimited healing potions before final battle
- Can afford multiple Raise Dead services (5,000 gp each)
- Final challenge loses tension if players can buy their way out of danger

**Considerations:**
- Late game should feel rewarding, not trivial
- Some wealth accumulation is appropriate for finale
- But 50% of campaign gold in final 20% is too much

**Recommended Fix Option A (Conservative):**
- Reduce Episode 9 dungeon loot: 7,450 → 4,500 gp (-2,950 gp)
- Reduce Episode 10 dungeon loot: 7,350 → 5,000 gp (-2,350 gp)
- Keep episode bonuses unchanged (thematic rewards)
- **New Episodes 9-10 Total:** 10,500 gp (33.5% of adjusted total)

**Recommended Fix Option B (Balanced):**
- Reduce Episode 9 dungeon loot: 7,450 → 5,000 gp (-2,450 gp)
- Reduce Episode 10 dungeon loot: 7,350 → 6,000 gp (-1,350 gp)
- **New Episodes 9-10 Total:** 12,000 gp (38.4% of adjusted total)
