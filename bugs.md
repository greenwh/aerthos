### Interpretation A: The "Half Weight" Rule (DMG p. 28)
This is the most commonly cited rule because it appears in the section specifically dedicated to armor and encumbrance.
*   **Weight:** Magic armor weighs **50%** of normal armor.
*   **Movement:** Magic armor improves your movement rate to the **next higher speed category**.

**How it works in practice:**
*   **Plate Mail:** Normally 6" movement. Magical Plate allows **9"** movement.
*   **Chain Mail:** Normally 9" movement. Magical Chain allows **12"** movement (the maximum for humans).
*   **Leather Armor:** Normally 12" movement. Magical Leather remains **12"** (it negates encumbrance but does not increase natural speed).
